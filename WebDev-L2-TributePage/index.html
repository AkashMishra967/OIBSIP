Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam

About Him

Dr. Avul Pakir Jainulabdeen Abdul Kalam was one of India's most respected scientists and leaders. He was born on 15 October 1931 in Rameswaram, Tamil Nadu.

Coming from a humble family, he worked hard to achieve his dreams. His interest in science and education helped him become one of India's most inspiring personalities.

He played an important role in India's space and missile development programmes. Because of his contributions, he became widely known as the Missile Man of India.

In 2002, he became the 11th President of India. Even after becoming President, he remained deeply connected with students and encouraged young people to dream big and work hard.
